Explainable guidance

Why should
I be careful?

Eira doesn't stop at a label. It points out the specific patterns that deserve attention.

Signal 01

Urgency language

The request creates pressure to act now. Scammers commonly use time pressure to reduce careful checking.

Signal 02

Request for payment

Money is requested before the sender's identity or claim has been independently confirmed.

Signal 03

Missing verification

A genuine request should be checked using contact details from an official source, not the message itself.

Verify & act

Safer next
steps.

1
Don't pay yet

Pause before sending money or sharing sensitive information.

2
Verify independently

Use the organization's official website or a known phone number.

3
Don't forward it

Avoid spreading the message until the claim is verified.

Remember

Eira gives guidance, not a guarantee. When money, accounts or safety are involved, verify through a trusted official source.
